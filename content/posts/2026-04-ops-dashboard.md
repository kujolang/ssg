# Why We Rebuilt Status Reporting

We moved from static updates to a dashboard because context was getting lost between teams.

## What We Changed

- Defined five non-negotiable service indicators.
- Added a release train widget for engineering and QA.
- Exposed incident notes directly next to deployment state.

## What Worked

The biggest win was reducing coordination lag between support and engineering.

> Fast feedback loops are usually an information architecture problem first.
